When the rent moves with sales

Property owners can report base rent and turnover rent separately

In results for the six months to September 2024, Link REIT separated base rent and turnover rent in its Hong Kong shop rental disclosure. That is an example of different rent components, not a quote or standard contract. The single premises and offers in this case are fictional.

Your classroom decision

Yan operates a Hong Kong mall kiosk and has two one-year renewal offers for the same location. Fixed is HK$42,000 per month. Turnover is HK$30,000 monthly plus 10% of monthly eligible sales above HK$250,000. The extra percentage applies only to the portion above the breakpoint.

Yan compares expected monthly cash rent for two equally likely scenarios. All operating receipts and costs other than rent are identical between the two offers under each scenario. Both contracts are affordable under the company’s cash rules; ignore risk preferences, tax, audit fees and month-to-month carryovers. Initially only the in-store sales forecast is counted in eligible turnover.

Your decision

Which lease has lower expected rent at first? Calculate cash rent in both scenarios and the eligible sales level at which the two monthly rents are equal.

Round 2 · More information

The term sheet now clarifies that completed delivery orders fulfilled by this kiosk also count, at the amount paid for goods after refunds but before platform commissions; delivery fees collected for someone else are not included. That adds HK$30,000 in eligible sales in the weak case and HK$90,000 in the strong case. These are existing sales, not newly generated orders, and no other assumption changes.

Your decision

Does the clarification change the choice? Compare the revised expected rents and name one record the parties need to settle the eligible-sales calculation.

Debrief · Apply the percentage only to the excess

Initially weak eligible sales are below HK$250,000, so Turnover rent is HK$30,000. Strong sales add 10% of HK$170,000, giving HK$47,000. Expected rent is HK$38,500, below HK$42,000. Choose Turnover under the original sales base.

Debrief · Find the month-by-month tie

For Turnover to reach Fixed it needs HK$12,000 more than its base. At 10%, that takes HK$120,000 of sales above the breakpoint. The rents tie at HK$370,000 in eligible sales; Fixed costs less for an isolated month above that figure.

Debrief · Change the base, not the rate

The clarified sales base is HK$210,000 or HK$510,000. Turnover remains HK$30,000 in weak months but reaches HK$56,000 in strong months. Its expected HK$43,000 is now HK$1,000 above Fixed, so choose Fixed under the new assumptions.

Debrief · Agree on what the metric measures

Specify which physical unit fulfilled orders and how refunds, commissions and third-party delivery fees are reconciled. Ask for a transaction-level platform settlement report mapped to kiosk order records. Do not substitute bank deposits for the agreed sales base without reconciling the deductions.

Instructor note

Allow 15 minutes: 2 to read, 4 for rents and threshold, 4 for the clarified sales base, and 5 to discuss evidence. Ask why the lower expected rent had no effect on the stated sales forecasts.
