## Required correction before final acceptance

The round-two statement that fourteen continuing users adapted the tray arrangement or wrote their own checklist does not, by itself, establish that the offer lacked a necessary complement. They might have customised a sufficient standard product for personal preferences. The debrief and instructor note currently treat their actions as supplying part of a missing complete solution.

Either:
- add bounded fictional evidence showing what setup gap those users actually filled, such as a documented missing sequence they supplied, while not claiming an experiment proves they could never have used the unadapted package; or
- qualify the inference: the early-user adaptations **may** have supplied part of the solution, and continuing use alone does not establish independent usability of the supplied package. The separate novice session already directly identifies an observed setup barrier.

Carry whichever correction is chosen into the reveal slide and debrief. Do not convert adaptations into proof that all early users are formal lead users. No other required factual or arithmetic corrections identified.
