## How a lead earns a place

1. Find a specific dated event, looking first in Hong Kong, then the rest of the Greater Bay Area, elsewhere in Asia, and farther afield when the story warrants it.
2. Try the opening vote aloud. Can students take opposing positions in the first minute and give a credible reason for each?
3. Find one fact worth revealing after the vote. It should change the trade-off or make students defend their original choice more carefully.
4. Follow the [authoring template](../authoring/case-template.md), including five distinct public sources, an original illustration, presentation and handout. Identify reported facts and invented classroom assumptions separately.
