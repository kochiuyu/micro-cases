# Cases 17–22 · Business news scouting plan

All six cases in this scouting round are published: [17](../sold-out.html), [18](../cash-flow.html), [19](../coupon.html), [20](../supervisor.html), [21](../distributor.html) and [22](../customer-notice.html). The original plan prioritized cash flow and a first-three review checkpoint. The owner approved continuing after the internal review, with classroom feedback still pending.

| Slot | Topic | First vote | Round-two reveal to look for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 17 (published) | Operations | The promotion sold out by lunch. Double tomorrow’s order? | A one-off event brought customers who usually shop elsewhere. |
| 18 (published) | Cash flow | Your biggest order yet is profitable. Accept it? | The customer pays in 90 days and payroll falls due next week. Negotiate a deposit or walk away? |
| 19 (published) | Marketing | Thousands used the coupon. Run the campaign again? | Many were regulars who would have paid full price. |
| 20 (published) | People management | Give the best salesperson the supervisor role? | Shift records show how the rest of the team performed under each candidate. |
| 21 (published) | Strategy | Grant an exclusive distributor the right to launch your brand across the border? | The proposed contract gives the distributor control of customer data and sales channels. |
| 22 (published) | Governance | Customers report a defect. What should the company say today? | New evidence changes which batches might be affected. Draft the updated customer message. |

For each lead, search Hong Kong first, followed by the rest of the Greater Bay Area, elsewhere in Asia, and then farther afield. Verify the event and clearly label any invented facts. Use the [case template](../authoring/case-template.md), including its five-source standard. Choose the story that generates the liveliest *reasoned* disagreement. A quieter topic with a terrific decision can take priority over a famous company with little for students to decide.
