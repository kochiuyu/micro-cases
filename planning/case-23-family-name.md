# Case 23 scouting brief · Would you sell the family name?

**Primary area:** Entrepreneurship, innovation and family business  
**Setting:** A family dessert business in Hong Kong  
**Status:** Published as [Case 23](../family-name.html), with editable material in [authoring](../authoring/family-name.md).

## Why this story

Hui Lau Shan began with a family selling herbal drinks and jelly in Yuen Long. An HKVCA retrospective records that Navis Capital acquired the entire family-owned business in 2007. In November 2021, Hong Kong outlets reported that the last local franchise, in Yau Tong, was preparing to close. Save that later headline for the debrief. It raises the question of what a family hopes to preserve when it sells. The sources do not establish that the sale caused the later closure.

## First vote

Your family runs a fictional Hong Kong mango-dessert business. Two buyers offer cash at completion. Buyer A offers HK$12 million for the business and exclusive use of the family name. Buyer B offers HK$10 million and will rebrand within a year. Both will retain the staff for the first year. Your family retains the same freedom to trade under a different name in either deal.

**Which buyer gets your signature? What would the family be buying for the HK$2 million it gives up by choosing B?**

## One fact changes

Buyer A will now accept a condition. For HK$11 million, the family can veto any change to the signature dessert sold under its name for five years. Store locations and future sale of the business remain the buyer’s decisions.

**Take HK$12 million, take HK$11 million with the veto, or take HK$10 million and reclaim the name? If the thing you care about is the original shop, what clause would you ask for instead?**

## How to run it

Give students thirty seconds to pick a bid. Pair a retiring owner who values cash with a next-generation relative who may want to use the name again. Ask each pair for one clause they would pay for, its maximum price, and who would check compliance. The HK$1 million price of the veto is visible. Its value depends on what the family wants to protect. A recipe veto cannot keep a shop open.

All offers, amounts, rights and family preferences are invented for teaching. The published historical sources do not disclose these terms for Hui Lau Shan.
