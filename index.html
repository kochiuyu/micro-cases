Teaching with a case

Choose Present to project the opening question. Ask students to commit to a choice and explain it to a neighbour. Reveal the changed fact in round two, then ask whose decision changed and why.

Open the debrief after the discussion. Each case includes an instructor note, sources, and a Print student handout button.
