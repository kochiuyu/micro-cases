# The next market. The next missing capability?

![Two packaging-design colleagues discuss a plain carton beside design samples and a planning folder.](../next-market-missing-capability-scene.jpg)

## Related technologies can support different businesses

On 22 January 2024, Fujifilm described its development into businesses including healthcare, materials, business innovation and imaging. Its healthcare overview also describes applications of expertise developed in photographic film. These are company accounts, not evidence that any adjacent market is easy to enter. The small Hong Kong design firm and all costs below are fictional.

## Opening decision

A fictional Hong Kong packaging-design studio has reliable routines for taking briefs, controlling artwork versions and approving print proofs. It sells design work; it does not currently promise to manage a client’s entire product launch. The owners have HK$120,000 for one expansion path this quarter, separate from fully funded existing work, staff pay and mandatory safeguards. They cannot run both paths with the available management attention. Nothing is committed.

Path A costs HK$80,000 to reach more local customers with the existing packaging-design and proofing service. It uses established routines and can be offered after four weeks of preparation. Path B costs HK$120,000 to develop a managed-launch service, coordinating design, printing and delivery milestones. It contains HK$40,000 for a fixed-term coordination specialist and HK$80,000 for team process development, rehearsal and readiness assessment. These are complete development budgets, not equipment prices or forecasts of profit.

A recent customer study identifies a larger pool of unmet requests for managed coordination than for design alone, but gives no reliable conversion rate or comparable profit forecast. Two prospective customers invite a managed-service proposal; neither has ordered. Both markets remain accessible next quarter. Path B must pass readiness and required legal, safety, privacy and supplier checks before accepting work. The studio may deliberately fund capability development this quarter without launching yet; unused Path A funds remain uncommitted.

**Student question:** Would you fund the nearby packaging expansion or the managed-launch adjacency this quarter? Identify what transfers from the current business and what the new offer would require.

## Round two

A paid capability assessment, completed before either expansion is chosen, distinguishes two gaps. An experienced coordination specialist is available within the HK$40,000 allocation. However, the studio has no shared routine for reconciling a changed artwork version with printer approval, delivery scheduling and client sign-off. In an internal rehearsal, each individual task was completed, but conflicting versions reached two teams because nobody owned the change handoff. No client work or personal data was involved.

The HK$80,000 process-development allocation already funds twelve weeks of joint workflow design, repeated rehearsals and an independent readiness assessment, with protected staff time. It does not buy a guaranteed pass. No safe accelerated launch or ready-made partner service has been validated. Path A’s four-week preparation and existing quality controls remain feasible. The two prospects are willing to reconsider a proposal after twelve weeks, without committing to buy; the reported unmet need has not disappeared. Both development budgets and the opportunity to choose either path remain unchanged.

**Student question:** With the capability map in hand, which path would you fund now? Explain why hiring the specialist does or does not settle the decision, and set a credible boundary on any launch promise.

## Related is not identical

The studio’s design knowledge, customer relationships and proofing discipline transfer to both paths. Managed coordination adds responsibility for the interactions between activities. Similar clients and materials do not establish that the organisation can reliably deliver the broader promise.

## A hire is a resource; a routine is coordinated action

The specialist can contribute expertise, but the rehearsal exposes a team-level handoff gap. Clear change ownership, common version records and repeated joint practice must work together. One person’s experience does not automatically create that shared operating capability. The twelve-week plan is funded development, not a certified result.

## Choose a development path, not a fictional certainty

Path A uses HK$80,000 and preserves HK$40,000 for later decisions, with a four-week preparation period. Path B uses HK$120,000 to pursue a broader opportunity, but cannot honestly promise launch before readiness is demonstrated. It can still be a rational investment in a new capability. Neither the larger request pool nor the lower development cost supplies a profit ranking; explain the firm’s strategic priority and its willingness to fund uncertain development.

## Instructor note

STR-04 changes product features to meet a foreign channel’s requirements. Here the question is corporate scope and the organisation’s ability to deliver a different service, not a product-size adjustment or a software purchase. The rehearsal is diagnostic fictional evidence, not a causal experiment. Mandatory readiness is protected under either choice; the meaningful decision is which complete development path to fund now. Do not treat Fujifilm’s history or an insurance-industry study as a prediction for a small studio.
