# The source withdrew the number. What can we still publish?

![Two staff check a worksheet and a proof in a fund communications office.](../the-source-withdrew-the-number-scene.jpg)

## Fund communications need evidence and context

Hong Kong’s Securities and Futures Commission publishes advertising guidance for authorised collective investment schemes. Hong Kong’s Investor and Financial Education Council distinguishes funds’ formal offering information from other investment information. This fictional pre-publication decision supplies its own policy and prescribes no real publication requirement.

## Opening decision

Mina prepares an optional one-page briefing for a fictional Hong Kong firm about an invented fund. It is not the offering document. The approved offering materials remain available, and no required disclosure or support is delayed. The briefing has not gone live and does not invite students to buy anything.

Internal policy requires supported claims, clear qualifiers and independent review of every retained module after changes. A shortened briefing is allowed only if the key facts and risk module can stand alone without an omitted claim. Omit an unsupported module; never assume an earlier review remains valid after edits.

A verification vendor initially confirms current evidence for all three modules. The same independent reviewer has 75 minutes before scheduled release. Use the exhibit’s sequential review times. If the validated full briefing fits, publish it. Otherwise publish the validated key facts and risk module alone when possible; hold the optional briefing if neither fits.

## Evidence

| Fictional internal module | Independent review time |
| --- | --- |
| Key facts and risk | 30 minutes |
| Past performance with its qualifier | 25 minutes |
| Service directory | 15 minutes |

**Student question:** Can the full briefing be published by the deadline? What must be established about the evidence and the version that actually goes live?

## Round two

The vendor withdraws the performance evidence and cannot replace it before the deadline. Removing performance from the release makes the service directory ineligible too: it points to the removed panel. Editing and checking the layout and cross-references now takes 20 minutes before the retained key facts and risk module receives independent review. Only 55 reviewer minutes remain in the same queue; treat all 20 edit-check minutes as using those minutes. The offering materials are unaffected.

**Student question:** What should Mina publish, if anything? Compute the time needed, identify what must be omitted and logged, and state the narrowest condition that would force a hold instead.

## Debrief · Capacity cannot substitute for evidence

The original review takes 30 + 25 + 15 = 70 minutes, leaving five. The planned full version fits only if the reviewer confirms support, the qualifiers remain visible and the exact released version matches the version reviewed.

## Debrief · Remove dependencies, not just the number

Performance is unsupported and the directory points to the omitted panel. Keep only the independently supportable facts-and-risk module. Cleanup and its review take 20 + 30 = 50 minutes; 55 leaves five. Keep the risk context attached and preserve the unaffected offering information.

## Debrief · Make the hold rule explicit

Hold if the trimmed version no longer stands alone with its qualifiers or if the queue falls below 50 minutes under the stated timings. At exactly 50 it just fits. Keep the evidence withdrawal, version, retained and omitted claims, verifier and sign-off in the internal record. Do not replace a missing number with a vague promotional claim.

## Instructor note

Use 2 minutes to read, 4 to scope evidence, 4 to recalculate queue times and 5 to discuss how partial release could mislead. This fictional policy is not investment advice or a legal view on required Hong Kong offering disclosures.
