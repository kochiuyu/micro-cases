# Automate the routine. Who keeps the exceptions?

<!-- legacy-case-id: 544 -->

ENT-45 · Hong Kong · 15 minutes · Process automation · Cost thresholds · Volume mix · Exception review

![Two office colleagues compare a row of matching blue folders with a thicker tabbed packet.](../keep-the-exceptions-manual-scene.jpg)

## A digital form can still need judgment

Hong Kong’s Trade Single Window user terms dated 1 May 2026 describe an electronic platform for registered users to submit trade documents and cargo information. The platform’s own FAQ also documents a function for copying details from a prior application. This fictional venture prepares drafts internally and does not submit anything to a government system.

## Opening decision

A Hong Kong startup prepares draft shipment document packs for small manufacturers. Ava can keep entering every pack manually or pay for a template tool that fills routine draft fields. All files still go through the same qualified release review. The tool cannot declare unfamiliar details correct or bypass that review.

Routine packs have complete inputs that match the template. Exceptions include missing information or inconsistent details and must go to a human. The proposed tool can flag the exceptions in this exercise, but does not reduce their cost or review. Both options can handle the whole month of work at the same turnaround and quality.

## Evidence

| Monthly inputs | Keep manual | Automate routine only |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine pack cost | HK$18 | HK$8 |
| Exception pack cost | HK$28 | HK$28 |
| Tool charge | HK$0 | HK$3,200 |
| Expected mix | 420 routine, 180 exceptions | 420 routine, 180 exceptions |

**Student question:** Should Ava use the template tool next month? Calculate both total costs and how many routine packs the tool must process to match the manual option.

## Round two

Ava checks the intake records for a prospective customer mix. The expected total is still 600 packs, but only 270 are routine. The remaining 330 are exceptions. No files are yet committed and prices and service quality stay fixed. There are no switching costs beyond the listed tool charge.

**Student question:** Would you make the same decision under the new mix? State one trigger for revisiting automation and one task you would keep under qualified review.

## Debrief · Count the files the tool can help

With 420 routine and 180 exception packs, manual costs 420 × HK$18 + 180 × HK$28 = HK$12,600. The template tool costs HK$3,200 + 420 × HK$8 + 180 × HK$28 = HK$11,600. Use the tool for the original workload, with review unchanged.

## Debrief · Use the break-even volume

Only routine packs save money. Each saves HK$10 against a HK$3,200 monthly cost. The options tie at 320 routine packs and the tool is cheaper from 321, regardless of exception volume under the model.

## Debrief · Same workload, different decision

For the revised intake, manual costs 270 × HK$18 + 330 × HK$28 = HK$14,100. Tool use costs HK$3,200 + 270 × HK$8 + 330 × HK$28 = HK$14,600. Keep manual entry, saving HK$500. Revisit once routine volume is reliably above 320 with review performance intact.

## Debrief · Count actual fit, not signups

Measure how many files arrive complete and match the template without corrections. Keep disputed or missing information with a qualified reviewer. A pilot can test misrouting, escalation workload and whether the same turnaround and quality really hold.

## Instructor note

Allow 15 minutes: 2 to read, 4 for the first cost comparison, 4 for the mix change, and 5 to plan a pilot. Ask who could label a borderline pack and whether a manual example justifies automating it.

## Sources and case assumptions · optional reference

All information needed for classroom decisions is provided above. Sources are optional reading.

All startup practices, volumes, tool capabilities and charges are fictional. The cited documents illustrate actual digital workflows only, not applicability of trade requirements or authority to file anything.

- [Hong Kong Trade Single Window, user terms dated 1 May 2026](https://www6.tradesinglewindow.hk/portal/en/public-services/registration/terms-and-conditions.html). Defines the government electronic platform and its registered-user purpose.

- [Hong Kong Trade Single Window, Licence / Permit](https://www5.tradesinglewindow.hk/portal/en/public-services/licence-permit/index.html). Distinguishes online submission and checking status or results.
- [Hong Kong Customs, Electronic Submission of Cargo Manifests](https://www.customs.gov.hk/hcms/filemanager/en/content_71/emanpamphlet_e.pdf). Describes a distinct document workflow and electronic responses, not assumptions about the fictional packs.
- [Hong Kong Customs, trade-facilitation FAQs](https://www.customs.gov.hk/en/service-enforcement-information/trade-facilitation/faqs/index.html). Shows that platforms and channels vary by document and use. This exercise does not advise on any particular product filing.
