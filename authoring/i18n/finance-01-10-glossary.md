# FIN-01–FIN-10 terminology

English is the fact source. Simplified Chinese is written for mainland teaching; traditional Chinese is written for Hong Kong. The two Chinese versions are drafted separately. Figures, case order, and legacy case numbers are unchanged.

Official pages: Hong Kong Deposit Protection Scheme (dps.org.hk, simplified and traditional), HKMA press release of 1 October 2024 on the HK$800,000 limit, and the Insurance Authority’s Chinese name 保險業監管局.

| English in the case | zh-cn | zh-hk | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Deposit Protection Scheme | 存款保障计划 | 存款保障計劃 | Official name. Limit is per depositor per member bank, not per account. |
| depositor / member bank | 存款人 / 成员银行 | 存戶 / 成員銀行 | Mainland pages say 存款人、账户; Hong Kong pages say 存戶、戶口、成員銀行. |
| protection ceiling | 保障上限 | 保障額 | Accrued interest sits inside the ceiling. |
| customer deposit on an order (FIN-03) | 订金 | 訂金 | A prepayment from a customer, not a bank deposit. |
| business interruption | 营业中断 | 業務中斷 | Insurance Authority and policy wording. |
| deductible / waiting period | 免赔额 / 等待期 | 自負額 / 等候期 | Local policy language. |
| basis risk | 基差风险 | 基礎風險 | The case defines it as a trigger that does not match the loss. Not a futures-basis lecture. |
| natural hedge | 自然对冲 | 自然對沖 | An operating receipt offsetting an operating payment in the same currency. |
| FX exposure | 风险敞口 | 外匯風險 | FIN-04’s point is that the invoice is larger than the unmatched amount. |
| expected shortfall (FIN-01) | 保障费盖不住的差额 | 保障費未能覆蓋的差額 | This is premium minus expected payout, not the risk measure Expected Shortfall. |
| financing cost (FIN-06) | 融资成本 | 融資成本 | Undiscounted all-in cost. The case forbids calling it an APR (实际年利率 / 實際年利率). |
| early-payment discount | 提前付款折扣 | 提早付款折扣 | Compared with what would have happened, not with the contractual due date. |
| bridging-finance cost | 过渡融资成本 | 過渡融資成本 | A supplied avoidable cost, not a bank quote. |
| simple payback | 简单回收期 | 簡單回本期 | Recovery timing only. Do not interpolate B to 2.67 years. |
| NPV / required return | 净现值 / 要求回报率 | 淨現值 / 要求回報率 | The supplied 10% is a required return, not a loan quote. |
| cash concentration | 资金归集 | 現金集中 | Keeping operating cash at one bank. |
| on-demand deposit | 活期存款 | 活期存款 | Same term; the safety questions differ. |
| dilution | 股权稀释 | 股權攤薄 | Listing practice in Hong Kong uses 攤薄. |
| percentage points | 个百分点 | 個百分點 | 70% versus 64% is six percentage points, not a 6% change. |
| ordinary shares | 普通股 | 普通股 | Equal economic and voting rights in the case. |

Names kept or localized on purpose: Messi is 梅西 / 美斯; Inter Miami is 国际迈阿密 / 國際邁阿密; VTech is 伟易达 / 偉易達; Ascentage Pharma is 亚盛医药 / 亞盛醫藥; Harbour Print Limited and LightBench stay in Latin script beside a Chinese description. Source-link titles stay in English. FIN-11’s title stays in English because that case is not in this set. Snow Town stays in English.

Legacy numbers kept as printed: Case 38 on FIN-04, Case 50 on FIN-05, and the instructor note on FIN-08 citing Case 7 (now FIN-02) and Case 62 (now FIN-06).

## English wording left unchanged, flagged for review

- FIN-01’s heading is “Who pays when the star sits out?” The registry title is “When the star sits out”.
- FIN-01 says “expected shortfall” and “break-even premium” for a ticket surcharge. The pages do not add a risk-measure disclaimer.
- FIN-03’s “profit” is incremental contribution before tax and finance. Negative cash is an unfunded requirement, not an actual overdraft.
- FIN-08 mixes “modeled” and “modelled”.
- FIN-10’s source note says “Phase 1 is complete and useful alone”. That means the phase is a self-contained service, not that the phase has already been finished.
