# ECO-01–ECO-50 terminology

Economics, competition and pricing. English is the fact source. Simplified Chinese is written for mainland teaching; traditional Chinese is written for Hong Kong. The two Chinese versions are drafted separately from the English. Figures, case order and legacy case numbers are unchanged. This glossary was drafted with the OpenAI Batch API (gpt-6.2-sol) from the 50 English cases, edited for the site conventions below, and used for every case in the area. Names marked unverified, and the flagged items at the end, still need faculty review.

Conventions carried over from Finance, Operations, Marketing and Accounting:

- Interface strings reuse the Finance wording: 全部案例 / 全部個案, 演示模式 / 簡報模式, 打印学生讲义 / 列印學生講義, 课堂决策 / 課堂決定, 你的决策 / 你的決定, 教师说明 / 教師說明, 第二轮 / 第二輪, 来源与案例假设 · 选读 / 來源與個案假設 · 選讀, 微型案例 / 微型個案, 上一个案例 / 上一個個案, 下一个案例 / 下一個個案. Area label: 经济学 / 經濟學.
- Classroom debrief headings and markers are 讲评 (zh-cn) and 講評 (zh-hk), exactly as on the Finance pages (讲评 · / 讲评： and 講評 · / 講評：). No variants: 复盘 / 復盤, 课后讨论 / 課後討論, 讨论总结 / 討論總結, 讲解 / 講解, 解说 / 解說 are not used for it (faculty decision, 28 Nov 2026).
- 讲评 / 講評 is only for the classroom debrief. A debrief inside the case story or its sources (a team's operational debrief, debrief research) is 事后回顾 / 事後回顧 or 回顾 / 回顧.
- HK$ amounts are written as the same figure followed by 港元 (HK$20 → 20港元). Other currencies keep their code or symbol as printed.
- Dates follow the Finance form on main after #13: 2024年7月29日, 2024年7月, 8月5日. A month standing alone with no year or day stays in characters (七月) so no figure is added. English number words, weekdays and ordinals stay as Chinese numerals; printed digits stay as digits.
- zh-hk is formal Hong Kong written Chinese (government releases, newspapers, university teaching notes), not a character conversion of zh-cn, not Taiwan usage and not colloquial Cantonese: 瓶頸 not 樽頸; 甚麼, 裏, 為, 線; 品質 for quality (zh-cn 质量); 資料 for information, except the fixed Hong Kong terms 資訊科技, 資訊系統, 資訊保安; 軟件, 網絡. 公里 is allowed. Other words containing 里 (e.g. milestone 里程碑, Delhi) are avoided in zh-hk because the language test bans that character outside 公里: milestone is 階段目標 and such names stay in Latin script.
- Source-link titles stay in English. Company, product and fictional names stay in Latin script unless an established Chinese name is listed below. Snow Town stays in English, as already decided on the Finance pages.

| congestion pricing / peak toll | 拥堵收费 / 高峰时段通行费 | 交通擠塞收費 / 繁忙時段隧道費 | ECO-01. Congestion pricing targets travel at congested times; the toll is only one part of the traveller’s cost. |
| generalised travel cost / break-even early-arrival cost | 广义出行成本 / 使两种时段成本相同的早到成本 | 廣義出行成本 / 令兩個時段成本相同的提早到達成本 | ECO-01. Include the toll, lost time at home and any valued queue time. Break-even here means indifference, not zero business profit. |
| sealed-bid auction / first-price and second-price sealed-bid auctions | 密封投标拍卖 / 第一价格与第二价格密封投标拍卖 | 密封投標拍賣 / 第一價與第二價密封投標拍賣 | ECO-02. The winner pays its own bid or the second-highest bid respectively. Distinguish both from the real fair’s open auction, 公开拍卖 / 公開拍賣. |
| private value / common value | 私人价值 / 共同价值 | 私人價值 / 共同價值 | ECO-02 and ECO-12. The stall has a bidder-specific value; the resale lot has the same eventual net value for both bidders, even when their estimates differ. |
| winner’s curse / conditional expected value | 赢家诅咒 / 条件期望值 | 贏家詛咒 / 條件期望值 | ECO-12. Ask what winning reveals about the rival’s estimate. Keep FIN’s established rendering of winner’s curse. |
| fee incidence / split fee / host-only fee | 费用归宿 / 房东与住客分担的服务费 / 仅向房东收取的服务费 | 費用負擔歸屬 / 由房東及住客分擔的服務費 / 只向房東收取的服務費 | ECO-03. Who is charged on paper does not determine who ultimately bears the fee once listings are repriced. |
| contribution / marginal contribution | 边际贡献 / 额外一次使用带来的边际贡献 | 邊際貢獻 / 額外使用一次帶來的邊際貢獻 | ECO-04, ECO-14, ECO-18 and ECO-22. Keep the OPS term: revenue less the stated variable costs, not total profit. For the next visit, make the incremental unit explicit. |
| network effects / local matching | 网络效应 / 本地匹配 | 網絡效應 / 本地配對 | ECO-05. More registered participants need not mean more feasible matches in the right place and time. Use 订单匹配 / 訂單配對 for orders. |
| comparative advantage / absolute advantage | 比较优势 / 绝对优势 | 比較優勢 / 絕對優勢 | ECO-06. Comparative advantage depends on lower opportunity cost, not simply being faster or more productive at a task. |
| opportunity cost | 机会成本 | 機會成本 | ECO-01 and ECO-06. The value of the best alternative forgone, including time or output rather than necessarily a cash payment. |
| negative externality / private cost / external cost / social cost | 负外部性 / 私人成本 / 外部成本 / 社会成本 | 負外部性 / 私人成本 / 外部成本 / 社會成本 | ECO-07 and ECO-30. Separate costs borne by the decision maker from harm to others; the social comparison includes both. Avoid treating compensation as an additional resource cost. |
| bundling / pure bundling / mixed bundling | 捆绑定价 / 纯捆绑 / 混合捆绑 | 捆綁定價 / 純捆綁 / 混合捆綁 | ECO-08. Pure bundling offers only the package; mixed bundling also allows standalone purchases. 套餐 is the offer, not the pricing strategy. |
| purchasing power / price index | 购买力 / 价格指数 | 購買力 / 價格指數 | ECO-09 and ECO-42. A broad index measures a basket; it does not tell the workshop how many unchanged kits its budget buys at current supplier prices. |
| nominal / real | 名义 / 实际 | 名義 / 實質 | ECO-09 and ECO-42. Keep the FIN distinction for inflation adjustment. For actual observed figures, use 实际 / 實際, not the HK inflation term 實質. |
| risk allocation / moral hazard | 风险分配 / 道德风险 | 風險分配 / 道德風險 | ECO-10. The quote can change who bears restoration costs and therefore the incentive for extra prevention. 道德風險 does not imply dishonesty or excuse unsafe handling. |
| marginal / incremental / average | 边际 / 增量 / 平均 | 邊際 / 增量 / 平均 | ECO-11 and ECO-23. The relevant change is from adding the next hour, order or hire; it need not equal a historical average. An increment may be a discrete block. |
| step cost / fixed cost | 阶梯式成本 / 固定成本 | 階梯式成本 / 固定成本 | ECO-11, ECO-36 and ECO-49. A step cost jumps at a threshold. A cost fixed over the stated range may still be avoidable or recoverable. |
| signalling / asymmetric information / workmanship guarantee | 信号传递 / 信息不对称 / 维修工艺保证 | 訊號傳遞 / 資料不對稱 / 維修手工品質保證 | ECO-13. A credible guarantee can be more costly to honour for low-quality providers. Keep it distinct from existing remedies and from the historical car warranty. |
| two-part tariff / access fee / usage charge | 两部制定价 / 准入费 / 按使用量收费 | 兩部制定價 / 入場費 / 按用量收費 | ECO-14 and ECO-22. Separate the fixed fee from the unit charge; in the studio use 月费 / 月費 for monthly access. Tariff here is a pricing structure, not a customs duty. |
| complementary goods / system pricing | 互补品 / 系统定价 | 互補品 / 系統定價 | ECO-15. Assess the printer and compatible consumables over the stated usage horizon; they need not be sold as a bundle. |
| costly search / value of information / stopping rule | 有成本的搜寻 / 信息价值 / 搜寻停止规则 | 有成本的搜尋 / 資料價值 / 停止搜尋規則 | ECO-16. Compare the expected benefit of a usable additional quotation with the cost of obtaining it. It is not the maximum possible saving. |
| coordination failure / complementary investment / conditional commitment | 协调失灵 / 互补性投资 / 附条件承诺 | 協調失靈 / 互補投資 / 附條件承諾 | ECO-17. Each module is valuable only with the other. Distinguish a commitment tied to the other party’s action from an unfunded expression of interest. |
| common-pool resource / depletion / replenishment | 公共池塘资源（可耗竭的共享资源） / 耗减 / 补充 | 共用資源（可耗竭） / 耗減 / 補充 | ECO-18. Use by one team reduces near-term availability to the other. Do not call it a non-rival public good or generalise the toy replenishment rule to fisheries. |
| binding price ceiling / excess demand / non-price allocation | 有约束力的价格上限 / 超额需求 / 非价格分配 | 具約束力的價格上限 / 超額需求 / 非價格分配 | ECO-19. At the ceiling more copies are wanted than are available; queueing or a draw allocates them without clearing the market by price. |
| merger efficiencies | 合并带来的效率收益 | 合併帶來的效率效益 | ECO-20. Net operating savings are not automatically realised, attributable only to the merger or passed on to customers. |
| customer substitution / diversion ratio | 顾客替代选择 / 转移率 | 顧客轉用其他供應商或渠道 / 轉移比率 | ECO-20 and ECO-47. Diversion tracks where lost customers or bookings go. Specify the origin, destination and denominator; it is not market share. |
| price discrimination / group pricing / resale arbitrage | 价格歧视 / 分组定价 / 转售套利 | 價格歧視 / 按組別定價 / 轉售套戥 | ECO-21. Group pricing uses observed eligibility; resale may undermine the separation. Ticket transfer alone does not establish a paid resale. |
| double marginalisation / markup / vertical contract | 双重边际化 / 加价 / 纵向合同 | 雙重邊際化 / 加價 / 縱向合約 | ECO-22. Successive firms each price above their relevant cost. Markup here is a price–cost increment, not necessarily a percentage or a retail margin. |
| employer market power / monopsony power / labour supply to a firm | 雇主市场势力 / 劳动力买方垄断势力 / 面向单个企业的劳动供给 | 僱主市場力量 / 勞動力買方壟斷力量 / 個別企業面對的勞動力供應 | ECO-23. A firm can face upward-sloping labour supply and have wage-setting power without being the only employer. |
| marginal hiring expenditure | 边际雇用支出 | 邊際聘用開支 | ECO-23. This is the change in total wage expenditure from the next hire. Count higher pay for existing workers only if the stated wage rule applies to them. |
| bargaining surplus / outside option / best alternative to a negotiated agreement (BATNA) | 谈判剩余 / 外部选择 / 谈判协议的最佳替代方案 | 談判盈餘 / 協議以外的選擇 / 未能達成協議時的最佳替代方案 | ECO-24. Incremental gains are measured against each party’s best feasible no-agreement option, not against zero or a stated demand. |
| durable goods / credible pricing commitment / strategic waiting | 耐用品 / 可信的定价承诺 / 策略性等待 | 耐用品 / 可信的定價承諾 / 策略性等待 | ECO-25. Future offers compete with current offers; waiting trades early use for a possible discount. A stated plan is not necessarily credible. |
| emissions cap / tradable allowances / free allocation | 排放总量上限 / 可交易排放配额 / 免费分配 | 排放總量上限 / 可買賣的排放配額 / 免費分配 | ECO-26. Trading redistributes allowances under the overall cap. Allowances are not offset credits, and free allocation does not make using them costless. |
| marginal abatement cost | 边际减排成本 | 邊際減排成本 | ECO-26. The cost of the next unit abated, not average abatement cost or the observed allowance price. |
| rent seeking / rent dissipation / contest design | 寻租 / 经济租金耗散 / 竞赛机制设计 | 尋租 / 租值消散 / 競賽機制設計 | ECO-27. Contest effort can use up the value of an existing prize. Rent is economic rent, not property rent; the toy result does not apply to productive effort generally. |
| present bias / time inconsistency / commitment device | 当下偏差 / 时间不一致性 / 承诺机制 | 當下偏誤 / 時間不一致性 / 承諾機制 | ECO-28. The same person may reverse a plan as a decision becomes immediate. A voluntary restriction may help follow the plan but sacrifices flexibility. |
| default effect / active choice / opt-in / opt-out | 默认选项效应 / 主动选择 / 主动加入 / 主动退出 | 預設選項效應 / 主動選擇 / 主動加入 / 主動退出 | ECO-29. Under opt-in, no action means non-participation; under opt-out, it means participation. An unchanged default is not proof of preference. |
| Coasean bargaining / transaction costs | 科斯式谈判 / 交易成本 | 高斯式議價 / 交易成本 | ECO-30. Include the costs of identifying parties, reaching agreement and enforcing it. Private agreement does not displace public protections. |
| adverse selection / costly quality verification | 逆向选择 / 有成本的质量核验 | 逆向選擇 / 須付出成本的品質核實 | ECO-31. A price based on average quality can drive better goods out before trade. The case assumes perfect verification, not a perfect real inspection service. |
| screening / self-selection | 筛选 / 自我选择 | 篩選 / 自我選擇 | ECO-32. The seller designs a menu and customers sort themselves by choice; they are not assigned to categories or profiled. |
| incentive compatibility / participation constraint | 激励相容 / 参与约束 | 誘因相容 / 參與約束 | ECO-32. The first compares the intended plan with other plans; the second compares participation with walking away. Check ties separately. |
| tax incidence / tax wedge / relative price elasticity | 税收归宿 / 税楔 / 供需价格弹性的相对大小 | 稅負歸屬 / 稅收楔子 / 供求價格彈性的相對大小 | ECO-33. The wedge is the gap between the buyer’s gross price and seller’s net price. Economic burden depends on relative responsiveness, not who remits the tax. |
| price taker / competitive equilibrium | 价格接受者 / 竞争均衡 | 價格接受者 / 競爭均衡 | ECO-33 and ECO-34. No individual trader controls price in these models. Equilibrium clears the stated market, unlike a comparison at a fixed posted price. |
| short-run equilibrium / long-run supply | 短期均衡 / 长期供给 | 短期均衡 / 長期供應 | ECO-34. Some inputs, such as capacity, are fixed in the short run; entry and capacity adjustment can change long-run supply. The horizons are case assumptions. |
| Cournot competition / best response / Nash equilibrium | 古诺竞争 / 最优反应 / 纳什均衡 | 古諾競爭 / 最佳回應 / 納什均衡 | ECO-35. The strategic choice is quantity. A best response to a fixed rival output is not necessarily a pair of mutual best responses. |
| consumer surplus / total surplus | 消费者剩余 / 总剩余 | 消費者盈餘 / 總盈餘 | ECO-35 and ECO-37. Consumer surplus is willingness to pay net of what is paid. Follow the case’s treatment of resource costs when summing gains. |
| entry cost / sunk cost / asset recoverability | 进入成本 / 沉没成本 / 资产可收回性 | 進入成本 / 沉沒成本 / 資產可收回性 | ECO-36. Upfront funding may buy an asset with resale value. Before entry, prospective irrecoverable expenditure matters; do not describe it as already sunk. |
| differentiated entry / business stealing / net social benefit | 差异化进入 / 客源争夺效应 / 社会净收益 | 差異化進入 / 客源轉移效應 / 社會淨效益 | ECO-37. A new location can create convenience benefits, but diverted sales are not all new social value. Business stealing is an economic effect, not theft. |
| information cascade / Bayesian updating / dependent observations | 信息瀑布 / 贝叶斯更新 / 非独立观测 | 資料瀑布效應 / 貝葉斯更新 / 非獨立觀察 | ECO-38. Instead of counting followers as separate signals, update beliefs using the information their choices actually reveal. |
| baseline / additionality | 基准线 / 额外性 | 基準線 / 額外性 | ECO-39. A measured fall relative to a reference level is not necessarily a reduction caused by the rebate. A fixed historical baseline need not be the no-policy counterfactual. |
