# IS-01–IS-50 terminology

Information systems, AI and business analytics. English is the fact source. Simplified Chinese is written for mainland teaching; traditional Chinese is written for Hong Kong. The two Chinese versions are drafted separately from the English. Figures, case order and legacy case numbers are unchanged. This glossary was drafted with the OpenAI Batch API (gpt-6.2-sol) from the 50 English cases, edited for the site conventions below, and used for every case in the area. Names marked unverified, and the flagged items at the end, still need faculty review.

Conventions carried over from Finance, Operations, Marketing and Accounting:

- Interface strings reuse the Finance wording: 全部案例 / 全部個案, 演示模式 / 簡報模式, 打印学生讲义 / 列印學生講義, 课堂决策 / 課堂決定, 你的决策 / 你的決定, 教师说明 / 教師說明, 第二轮 / 第二輪, 来源与案例假设 · 选读 / 來源與個案假設 · 選讀, 微型案例 / 微型個案, 上一个案例 / 上一個個案, 下一个案例 / 下一個個案. Area label: 信息系统 / 資訊系統.
- Classroom debrief headings and markers are 讲评 (zh-cn) and 講評 (zh-hk), exactly as on the Finance pages (讲评 · / 讲评： and 講評 · / 講評：). No variants: 复盘 / 復盤, 课后讨论 / 課後討論, 讨论总结 / 討論總結, 讲解 / 講解, 解说 / 解說 are not used for it (faculty decision, 28 Nov 2026).
- 讲评 / 講評 is only for the classroom debrief. A debrief inside the case story or its sources (a team's operational debrief, debrief research) is 事后回顾 / 事後回顧 or 回顾 / 回顧.
- HK$ amounts are written as the same figure followed by 港元 (HK$20 → 20港元). Other currencies keep their code or symbol as printed.
- Dates follow the Finance form on main after #13: 2024年7月29日, 2024年7月, 8月5日. A month standing alone with no year or day stays in characters (七月) so no figure is added. English number words, weekdays and ordinals stay as Chinese numerals; printed digits stay as digits.
- zh-hk is formal Hong Kong written Chinese (government releases, newspapers, university teaching notes), not a character conversion of zh-cn, not Taiwan usage and not colloquial Cantonese: 瓶頸 not 樽頸; 甚麼, 裏, 為, 線; 品質 for quality (zh-cn 质量); 資料 for information, except the fixed Hong Kong terms 資訊科技, 資訊系統, 資訊保安; 軟件, 網絡. 公里 is allowed. Other words containing 里 (e.g. milestone 里程碑, Delhi) are avoided in zh-hk because the language test bans that character outside 公里: milestone is 階段目標 and such names stay in Latin script.
- Source-link titles stay in English. Company, product and fictional names stay in Latin script unless an established Chinese name is listed below. Snow Town stays in English, as already decided on the Finance pages.

- IS-01: Confirm where the full case says IPO and where it only says listing. Do not turn illiquid holdings into realised proceeds before a sale, or turn two falling series into a causal result.
- IS-02: The Consumer Council account is a complaint, not the source of the 97% figure or evidence that AI invented a policy. Use 8号烈风或暴风信号 / 8號烈風或暴風信號 if the full signal name is needed; preserve the source digit.
- IS-03, IS-19, IS-40: Distinguish a successful backup, a tested restore, resumed business work and reversal of downstream effects. Confirm which of these the source actually says was demonstrated.
- IS-04, IS-17, IS-36: Approval, authentication, authorisation and independent verification are separate controls. Two colleagues checking the same source, or a recipient possessing a link, do not by themselves establish independent verification or recipient identity.
- IS-05: Verify whether shipment advice is the generic document type or an exact ezTRADE message label. Use 发运通知 / 付運通知 for the generic type; do not attribute the fictional unit-conversion failure to OCBC Hong Kong or GS1 Hong Kong.
- IS-06, IS-07, IS-08: Check the full source notes for the placement and exact titles of WISE2025, Practical AI, AI for All, AI and Digital Transformation Zone, and Practice Guide on Cloud Adoption. The extracted list does not establish that these names refer to one initiative or that a Chinese title is official. Keep source-link titles in English.
- IS-06, IS-16, IS-33: Freeze each forecast target as stated: completed purchase transactions in IS-06, requested print jobs in IS-16, and eligible parcel requests in IS-33. Do not substitute visitors, fulfilled sales or accepted requests.
- IS-09, IS-25, IS-43, IS-45: Label the relevant clock and cutoff explicitly. A new display refresh, a successful feed retrieval, a later record arrival and a current estimate are different things. In IS-43, separate data-generation time from predicted bus-arrival time.
- IS-10, IS-22, IS-29, IS-32: Name the allocation and analysis units and distinguish offering, actual use and observed outcome. In IS-22, version 13 is selected from the initial comparison, not independently confirmed.
- IS-11, IS-12, IS-13: Verify denominators and population labels: respondents are not all customers; missing times are not zero; within-group changes and an aggregate reversal answer different questions. Confirm the expansion of BTS from its citation before translating it.
- IS-14, IS-27, IS-46: Precision takes different translations: 查准率 / 精確率 for classification versus 精密度 for measurement. Classification recall is 查全率 / 召回率, not the product-recall sense fixed in Operations.
- IS-15: Retain ART and verify its official expansion and any bilingual framework title against the cited HKMA announcement. Do not infer an expansion from the case summary or present banking guidance as a retail rule.
- IS-16, IS-17: Verify the bilingual titles of AI Readiness in Workplace Survey and Hong Kong Cyber Security Outlook against the HKPC and HKCERT releases. Keep the supplied English in citations; do not promote a descriptive translation to an official title.
- IS-20: Verify whether ONS supplies a Chinese title for Living Costs and Food Survey. The working description is not an official title. The dashboard threshold is a management rule, not evidence of a causal lift.
- IS-21, IS-32, IS-33: Distinguish statistical significance, practical importance and empirical prediction coverage. Confirm whether a displayed limit is one-sided before calling it a prediction interval.
- IS-24: Preserve Harbor Desk and Harbour Desk exactly as printed, including the spelling difference. Also preserve legal-entity identifiers and name variants; do not standardise them before students assess linkage.
- IS-26, IS-39: Distinguish an operation retry from a second intended order, and a payment attempt from a completed checkout. Keep request keys and synthetic checkout identifiers exactly as printed.
- IS-30: Do not conflate read-only access with permission to disclose every retrieved record. Check the publisher and jurisdiction behind any shorthand guidance citation, especially HK Government InfoSec, NZ NCSC, NIST and OWASP; do not assign a title or expansion by inference.
- IS-34, IS-35, IS-37: Keep T30/T90, customer split identifiers and R1/R2 unchanged. A changed target, a repeated customer and a changed deployment label are three different comparability or traceability problems.
- IS-38: Zero displays means the click rate is undefined, not zero. Repair run-together source-note text only after faculty confirm the intended counts, allocation and update rule.
- IS-39, IS-41, IS-42: Do not infer business events from record counts or order of receipt. In IS-41 the ledger shows proposed fees, not invoices; in IS-42 verify whether East Hall and West Hall are display names or protected fixture labels.

- IS-46: Do not attribute automated classification to the Consumer Council. Separate a changed category definition, a classifier’s errors and a real change in the underlying complaint count.
- IS-49: Require the target screen and action before translating any reused string. Preserve keys, protected variables and build identifiers; verify whether Translate, Localization Note and Localization Quality Issue are exact specification labels rather than ordinary prose.

- IS-01–IS-50: Treat the capitalised extraction as a candidate list, not a list of verified names. Discard sentence fragments. Keep source-link titles in English, preserve printed IDs and figures, and apply the site’s 港元, date and interface wording conventions.
