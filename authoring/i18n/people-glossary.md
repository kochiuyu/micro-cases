# PPL-01–PPL-50 terminology

People, leadership and organisational behaviour. English is the fact source. Simplified Chinese is written for mainland teaching; traditional Chinese is written for Hong Kong. The two Chinese versions are drafted separately from the English. Figures, case order and legacy case numbers are unchanged. This glossary was drafted with the OpenAI Batch API (gpt-6.2-sol) from the 50 English cases, edited for the site conventions below, and used for every case in the area. Names marked unverified, and the flagged items at the end, still need faculty review.

Conventions carried over from Finance, Operations, Marketing and Accounting:

- Interface strings reuse the Finance wording: 全部案例 / 全部個案, 演示模式 / 簡報模式, 打印学生讲义 / 列印學生講義, 课堂决策 / 課堂決定, 你的决策 / 你的決定, 教师说明 / 教師說明, 第二轮 / 第二輪, 来源与案例假设 · 选读 / 來源與個案假設 · 選讀, 微型案例 / 微型個案, 上一个案例 / 上一個個案, 下一个案例 / 下一個個案. Area label: 人力与组织 / 人力與組織.
- Classroom debrief headings and markers are 讲评 (zh-cn) and 講評 (zh-hk), exactly as on the Finance pages (讲评 · / 讲评： and 講評 · / 講評：). No variants: 复盘 / 復盤, 课后讨论 / 課後討論, 讨论总结 / 討論總結, 讲解 / 講解, 解说 / 解說 are not used for it (faculty decision, 28 Nov 2026).
- 讲评 / 講評 is only for the classroom debrief. A debrief inside the case story or its sources (a team's operational debrief, debrief research) is 事后回顾 / 事後回顧 or 回顾 / 回顧.
- HK$ amounts are written as the same figure followed by 港元 (HK$20 → 20港元). Other currencies keep their code or symbol as printed.
- Dates follow the Finance form on main after #13: 2024年7月29日, 2024年7月, 8月5日. A month standing alone with no year or day stays in characters (七月) so no figure is added. English number words, weekdays and ordinals stay as Chinese numerals; printed digits stay as digits.
- zh-hk is formal Hong Kong written Chinese (government releases, newspapers, university teaching notes), not a character conversion of zh-cn, not Taiwan usage and not colloquial Cantonese: 瓶頸 not 樽頸; 甚麼, 裏, 為, 線; 品質 for quality (zh-cn 质量); 資料 for information, except the fixed Hong Kong terms 資訊科技, 資訊系統, 資訊保安; 軟件, 網絡. 公里 is allowed. Other words containing 里 (e.g. milestone 里程碑, Delhi) are avoided in zh-hk because the language test bans that character outside 公里: milestone is 階段目標 and such names stay in Latin script.
- Source-link titles stay in English. Company, product and fictional names stay in Latin script unless an established Chinese name is listed below. Snow Town stays in English, as already decided on the Finance pages.

## Flagged for faculty review

- PPL-01: 公务员考试 / 公務員考試 refers to the mainland setting. Do not infer that Lin is taking the national rather than a local examination, or substitute a Hong Kong recruitment test.
- PPL-02: Use the brand styling foodpanda in running prose. Do not construct a Chinese company name from Hong Kong Foodpanda or substitute 熊猫外卖 / 熊貓外賣, which could identify another platform.
- PPL-03: CEPR can name two different research organisations; the PPL-03 source is the Centre for Economic Policy Research. Treat the article's firms and the fictional Hong Kong shop as separate evidence.
- PPL-04, PPL-05, PPL-16: No Chinese programme or event title is verified here for Career Launchpad, Work Improvement Team Convention or FutureSkills. Keep the printed Latin forms and check the relevant source mastheads. Confirm which case actually cites the Convention. PPL-04 also opens with a comma in the supplied excerpt; do not reconstruct the missing lead-in or date.
- PPL-05, PPL-43: Reporting more concerns is not the same as making more mistakes, feeling safer speaking up or resolving more concerns. Keep the count, underlying incidence and follow-through measures separate. For quality, use 质量 / 品質.
- PPL-06: Use 攜程集團 for the company in Hong Kong and Trip.com for the brand where that is the reference. Verify whether the source uses James Liang or an author byline with a Chinese name before changing it; do not infer a name from the capitalisation list.
