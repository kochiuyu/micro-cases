# STR-01–STR-50 terminology

Strategy and international business. English is the fact source. Simplified Chinese is written for mainland teaching; traditional Chinese is written for Hong Kong. The two Chinese versions are drafted separately from the English. Figures, case order and legacy case numbers are unchanged. This glossary was drafted with the OpenAI Batch API (gpt-6.2-sol) from the 50 English cases, edited for the site conventions below, and used for every case in the area. Names marked unverified, and the flagged items at the end, still need faculty review.

Conventions carried over from Finance, Operations, Marketing and Accounting:

- Interface strings reuse the Finance wording: 全部案例 / 全部個案, 演示模式 / 簡報模式, 打印学生讲义 / 列印學生講義, 课堂决策 / 課堂決定, 你的决策 / 你的決定, 教师说明 / 教師說明, 第二轮 / 第二輪, 来源与案例假设 · 选读 / 來源與個案假設 · 選讀, 微型案例 / 微型個案, 上一个案例 / 上一個個案, 下一个案例 / 下一個個案. Area label: 战略 / 策略.
- Classroom debrief headings and markers are 讲评 (zh-cn) and 講評 (zh-hk), exactly as on the Finance pages (讲评 · / 讲评： and 講評 · / 講評：). No variants: 复盘 / 復盤, 课后讨论 / 課後討論, 讨论总结 / 討論總結, 讲解 / 講解, 解说 / 解說 are not used for it (faculty decision, 28 Nov 2026).
- 讲评 / 講評 is only for the classroom debrief. A debrief inside the case story or its sources (a team's operational debrief, debrief research) is 事后回顾 / 事後回顧 or 回顾 / 回顧.
- HK$ amounts are written as the same figure followed by 港元 (HK$20 → 20港元). Other currencies keep their code or symbol as printed.
- Dates follow the Finance form on main after #13: 2024年7月29日, 2024年7月, 8月5日. A month standing alone with no year or day stays in characters (七月) so no figure is added. English number words, weekdays and ordinals stay as Chinese numerals; printed digits stay as digits.
- zh-hk is formal Hong Kong written Chinese (government releases, newspapers, university teaching notes), not a character conversion of zh-cn, not Taiwan usage and not colloquial Cantonese: 瓶頸 not 樽頸; 甚麼, 裏, 為, 線; 品質 for quality (zh-cn 质量); 資料 for information, except the fixed Hong Kong terms 資訊科技, 資訊系統, 資訊保安; 軟件, 網絡. 公里 is allowed. Other words containing 里 (e.g. milestone 里程碑, Delhi) are avoided in zh-hk because the language test bans that character outside 公里: milestone is 階段目標 and such names stay in Latin script.
- Source-link titles stay in English. Company, product and fictional names stay in Latin script unless an established Chinese name is listed below. Snow Town stays in English, as already decided on the Finance pages.

| English in the case | zh-cn | zh-hk | Why |

| strategic pricing / price matching | 战略定价／匹配对手价格 | 策略定價／跟隨對手定價 | STR-01: the payoff can depend on both firms' prices. Matching a price or discount is not necessarily the profit-maximising response. |
| Nash equilibrium / best response | 纳什均衡／最优反应 | 納什均衡／最佳回應 | An equilibrium requires mutual best responses. The best response to West's announced price alone does not establish equilibrium. |
| exclusive distribution rights / channel control | 独家经销权／渠道控制 | 獨家分銷權／渠道控制 | STR-02: the grant has a territory, term and channel scope. It does not transfer brand ownership or necessarily give the distributor control of every customer relationship. |
| make or buy / outsourcing | 自制或外购／外包 | 自製或外購／外判 | STR-03 separates repeat production from prototyping. Do not imply that outsourcing production requires outsourcing design or cutting staff. |
| core capabilities / organisational routines | 核心能力／组织惯例 | 核心能力／組織常規 | A capability is what the firm can reliably do; routines are repeated ways of working that may underpin it. Neither is simply an item of equipment. |
| standardisation / product adaptation | 标准化／产品适应性调整 | 標準化／產品調適 | STR-04: the firm may standardise its service promise and operating controls while adapting product features. Use 本地化 only where a local-market adaptation is intended. |
| acquisition rationale / synergies | 收购动因／协同效应 | 收購理據／協同效應 | STR-05: use 并购动因／併購理據 if the reference covers mergers as well as acquisitions. A stated rationale is not a realised benefit, and overlapping clients are not all incremental. |
| coopetition / contribution governance | 竞合／投入与使用规则 | 競合／投入及使用規則 | STR-06 and STR-24: cooperation is limited to defined activities. Here contribution means what partners provide and who may use the result, not unit contribution or legal clearance. |
| business-format franchising / company-owned outlet | 商业模式特许经营／直营门店 | 業務模式特許經營／直營分店 | STR-07 licenses a business system to independent operators, not just a sign or trade mark. This is not the statutory bus-franchise sense in STR-41. |
| scenario planning / forecast / strategic flexibility | 情景规划／预测／战略灵活性 | 情景規劃／預測／策略靈活性 | STR-08 explores multiple plausible futures rather than making one forecast. Flexibility means retaining useful options, not funding every option now. |
| positioning / differentiation | 市场定位／差异化 | 市場定位／差異化 | Positioning identifies whom the firm serves and what it offers; differentiation is what makes the offer meaningfully distinct. A higher price by itself establishes neither. |
| activity fit / strategic trade-off | 活动配称／战略取舍 | 營運活動配合／策略取捨 | STR-09: menu, preparation, packing and collection reinforce one another. A premium add-on may require a different set of activities. |
| customer concentration / renewal | 客户集中度／续约 | 客戶集中度／續約 | STR-10 and STR-17: concentration concerns dependence on a few accounts. Repeat purchases are not guaranteed renewals; where no contract is being renewed, translate renewal by the actual purchase decision. |
| product portfolio / managed harvest | 产品组合／有计划的收割策略 | 產品組合／有計劃的收割策略 | STR-11: reduce discretionary reinvestment while continuing to serve existing commitments. Harvest is not immediate withdrawal, a support cut or a closure. |
| asset specificity / redeployability | 资产专用性／资产可转用性 | 資產專用性／資產可轉用性 | STR-12 and STR-41: consider the asset's value in another use or relationship. Physical portability does not guarantee usable rights, viable redeployment or resale value. |
| contractual safeguards / relationship governance | 合同保障条款／交易关系治理 | 合約保障條款／交易關係管治 | STR-12: a binding minimum purchase commitment is a safeguard; a forecast is not. Relationship governance also includes how parties coordinate and address changes. |
| entry timing / first-mover advantage | 进入时机／先发优势 | 進入時機／先行者優勢 | STR-13: being first to announce, take reservations or open are different milestones. A first-mover advantage must come from a benefit, not just an earlier date. |
| equity entry mode / wholly owned operation / equity joint venture | 股权进入模式／全资经营／股权合资企业 | 股權式市場進入模式／全資經營／股權合營企業 | STR-14 compares ownership structures. Translate shared control as 共同控制 and explain the actual decision rights; do not imply a 50:50 holding or a formal accounting conclusion. |
| geographic scope / operating density | 地理范围／网点密度 | 地域範圍／分店密度 | STR-15 separates spreading across markets from clustering outlets so they can share operating support. More outlets do not necessarily mean denser operations. |
| ecosystem strategy / compatibility / interoperability | 生态系统战略／兼容性／互操作性 | 生態系統策略／兼容性／互操作性 | STR-16: compatibility is the ability to work with a specified system; interoperability concerns devices or systems exchanging and using information. A common standard need not support every distinctive feature. |
| competitive response / segment economics | 竞争应对／细分市场的收益与成本 | 競爭應對／細分市場的收益與成本 | STR-17: judge price matching by account economics and service costs within each segment, not retention alone. |
| substitution / competitive boundaries | 替代／竞争范围 | 替代／競爭範圍 | STR-18: an alternative way to meet the same customer need may be a substitute without being a direct industry rival. This is not a formal competition-law market definition. |
| related diversification / economies of scope | 相关多元化／范围经济 | 相關多元化／範圍經濟 | STR-19 and STR-40: diversification is the choice of business scope; economies of scope arise when providing different outputs together costs less than providing them separately. |
| capability transfer / transferability | 能力转移／可转移性 | 能力轉移／可轉移性 | STR-19, STR-26, STR-40 and STR-41: specify what transfers, to whom, and with which supporting routines or rights. Do not imply that accreditation or permissions follow an asset. |
| decision rights / decentralisation | 决策权／分权 | 決策權／下放決策權 | STR-20: delegation lets a branch decide within agreed limits; it does not remove shared production constraints or central responsibility for standards. |
| emergent strategy / deliberate strategy | 涌现战略／预定战略 | 湧現策略／預定策略 | STR-21: an emergent strategy is a pattern recognised in action rather than one executed as originally intended. It is not an emergency response. |
| strategic learning / pivot | 战略学习／战略转向 | 策略學習／策略轉向 | STR-21 and STR-29: learning updates assumptions from evidence. A pivot is a substantive change of direction, not every adjustment after a trial. |
| resource-based view / imitability | 资源基础观／可模仿性 | 資源基礎觀／可模仿性 | STR-22: a visible product can be copied without reproducing partner relationships, routines or delivery capability. Hard to imitate does not mean permanently profitable. |
| corporate parenting / parenting advantage | 母公司管理／母公司增值优势 | 母公司管理／母公司的增值優勢 | STR-23 asks what this parent adds relative to another owner or standalone operation, not simply whether the division under it makes a profit. |
| portfolio fit / divestment | 业务组合契合度／业务剥离 | 業務組合的配合程度／業務剝離 | STR-23: use 出售业务／出售業務 if a sale is specifically meant. A review is not a decision, and a separation does not necessarily mean a sale. |
| value creation / value capture (value appropriation) | 价值创造／价值获取 | 價值創造／價值獲取 | STR-24, STR-25, STR-38 and STR-48: distinguish the total value created from the share a participant retains. Appropriation here does not imply misappropriation. |
| complementary assets / complementors | 互补性资产／互补者 | 互補資產／互補者 | STR-25, STR-38 and STR-48: installation, distribution or interface access can help commercialise the offer. A complementor is the party supplying a complementary product or service, not the asset itself. |
| partner incentives / access terms | 合作伙伴激励／接入条件 | 合作夥伴的誘因／接入條件 | STR-25 and STR-38: incentives influence what partners actually do; access terms govern qualification, use and payment. For access to a distribution channel, use 渠道准入条件／使用渠道的條件. |
| reverse knowledge transfer / subsidiary innovation | 逆向知识转移／子公司创新 | 逆向知識轉移／附屬公司創新 | STR-26: knowledge moves from an overseas subsidiary to headquarters. Sending a design is not proof that the receiving team can interpret or apply it. |
| post-acquisition integration / strategic interdependence / organisational autonomy | 收购后整合／战略相互依赖／组织自主性 | 收購後整合／策略上的相互依存／組織自主性 | STR-27: the value of coordination may differ by activity. Common ownership is settled and does not require immediate uniformity in operating routines. |
| strategic groups / mobility barriers | 战略群组／流动壁垒 | 策略群組／轉換群組的障礙 | STR-28: barriers impede movement between strategic groups within an industry. They are not necessarily barriers to entering that industry or rules on staff mobility. |
| organisational ambidexterity / exploration / exploitation | 组织双元性／探索／利用 | 組織雙元性／探索／利用 | STR-29: exploration tests uncertain opportunities; exploitation improves and uses established capabilities. Exploitation does not mean 剥削／剝削. |
| dynamic capabilities | 动态能力 | 動態能力 | STR-30: the capacity to sense change, seize opportunities and reconfigure resources and routines. Distinguish it from the operating capability that delivers today's work. |
| channel migration / learning lead time | 渠道迁移／学习提前期 | 渠道轉移／學習前置時間 | STR-30: orders move to a different channel. The lead time is the interval needed to build dependable capability, not a supplier's delivery deadline. |
| platform governance / boundary resources / complementary innovation | 平台治理／边界资源／互补创新 | 平台管治／邊界資源／互補創新 | STR-31: interfaces, development tools and participation rules both enable and constrain third-party extensions. Opening an extension point is not giving up control of the core. |
| multi-homing / network effects | 多平台参与／网络效应 | 多平台參與／網絡效應 | STR-32 and STR-44: multi-homing means participants can use more than one platform. Network effects concern how participation affects value to users; a larger sign-up count alone does not establish them. |
| economies of scale / capacity utilisation | 规模经济／产能利用率 | 規模經濟／產能使用率 | STR-33: separate the cost effect of operating at a different scale from spreading fixed costs by using more of the same plant's capacity. |
| learning effects / experience curve | 学习效应／经验曲线 | 學習效應／經驗曲線 | STR-33: compare experience accumulated over time with the current period's output rate. Do not assume that cheaper inputs or a larger factory demonstrate learning. |
| credible commitment / sequential decision | 可信承诺／序贯决策 | 可信承諾／序列決策 | STR-34: the promised action must still be in the firm's interest when its turn arrives. A threat alone does not change those incentives. |
| sunk cost / irreversible capacity investment | 沉没成本／不可逆的产能投资 | 沉沒成本／不可逆轉的產能投資 | STR-34: evaluate an avoidable investment before committing. After commitment, only costs that cannot be recovered are sunk. |
| institutional gaps / credible verification | 制度空缺／可信核验 | 制度缺口／可信核實 | STR-35: identify a missing market function, such as a way for buyers to check a supplier's capability. Prominence, client references, verified claims and blanket approval are not equivalent. |
| alliance portfolio / strategic complementarity | 联盟组合／战略互补性 | 聯盟組合／策略互補性 | STR-36: separate each direct alliance's standalone value from how it supports the firm's shared product strategy and other partnerships. |
| product architecture / modularity | 产品架构／模块化 | 產品架構／模組化 | STR-36 and STR-50: architecture describes how components and interfaces fit together. A replaceable module can change eligibility or partner fit without changing the whole product. |
| servitisation / availability promise | 制造业服务化／设备可用性承诺 | 製造業服務化／設備可用性承諾 | STR-37: the manufacturer adds a service or availability obligation. A different billing schedule alone is not servitisation, and equipment availability is not a guarantee of customer profit. |
| risk allocation / use-based pricing | 风险分配／按使用量计费 | 風險分配／按使用量收費 | STR-37: specify who bears utilisation, downtime and support risks. Charging by use does not automatically transfer every operating risk. |
| multidimensional distance / operating adaptation | 多维距离／运营调整 | 多維距離／營運調整 | STR-39: compare actual language, administrative, geographic and economic frictions as relevant. Adapt the operating process on the evidence, not on assumptions about nationality. |
| accreditation / certification / qualification | 认可／认证／资格审核 | 認可／認證／資格審核 | STR-25, STR-38, STR-40, STR-45 and STR-50: laboratory accreditation has a defined scope; certification attests to specified requirements; buyer qualification is an eligibility check. Use 合格 or 符合资格／符合資格 when describing an input, not the review process. |
| public bus franchise / retail concession | 公共巴士专营权／店铺经营权 | 公共巴士專營權／店舖經營權 | STR-41 concerns a statutory franchise. STR-48 concerns a limited right to operate a museum shop; do not infer statutory exclusivity or a specific licence form. |
| complementary rights / staged commitment | 互补性权利／分阶段承诺 | 互補權利／分階段承諾 | STR-41: useful software or equipment may require separate access, operating or use rights. Staging holds some commitments until a later decision point. |
| competitive signalling / open methodology | 竞争信号传递／公开评分方法 | 競爭訊號傳遞／公開評分方法 | STR-42: publishing the method lets outsiders scrutinise or replicate it. Publishing a score without the method is different, and neither creates official certification. |
