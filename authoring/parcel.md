# Pay per parcel?

Case 15 · Hong Kong · 10–15 minutes · Incentive pay · Multitasking

![A courier and depot supervisor inspect a parcel at dispatch.](../parcel-scene.jpg)

## Hong Kong in the news · November 2021

Hong Kong Foodpanda couriers went on strike over grievances including pay changes and time spent waiting at restaurants. A payment per completed order rewards some parts of the job while leaving others unpaid. How would you design the contract?

## Opening decision

You manage a depot. Choose a payment rule for a one-hour shift. Couriers can make 8 careful deliveries or 10 hurried ones, at the same personal effort. They keep their pay for each recorded delivery.

## Evidence

| One shift, HK$ | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Hourly wage | 80 |
| Alternative piece rate per delivery | 9 |
| Revenue per recorded delivery | 12 |

**Your decision** Which pace does piece pay reward? How do hourly and piece pay compare for the courier at each pace?

## Round two

Next-day inspections show that hurried delivery causes two extra damage claims per shift, costing the depot HK$25 each. Careful delivery produces none. Drivers currently bear none of this cost.

**Your decision** Under piece pay, do the driver and depot prefer the same pace? Propose a payment rule and a quality measure.

## Debrief

Piece pay yields 72 for careful work and 90 for hurried work. The hourly wage gives 80 either way. Under piece pay the depot earns 8 × (12 − 9) = 24 at the careful pace, but 10 × (12 − 9) − 50 = −20 at the hurried pace.

## Instructor note

Minutes 0–3: vote and compare reasons in pairs. Minutes 3–7: reveal the changed fact and vote again. Minutes 7–12: reconcile the answers. Try an HK$80 base plus HK$12 for a verified damage-free shift. Under the stated outcomes careful work pays 92 versus 80. Ask who verifies damage, when bonus payment happens, and how to handle a parcel damaged before loading.
