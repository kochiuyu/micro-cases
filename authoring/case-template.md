## Opening decision

Give students a role, a concrete choice and a reason to decide now. Save the explanation for the debrief.

## Evidence

Use one legible exhibit. Label the period, units, geography and baseline. Trace each observation to an online source in the final section. Use the original cartoon to introduce the decision. Keep numerical evidence in a separate table or chart.

**Student question** [Ask for a choice and one piece of evidence.]

## Round two

Reveal one changed fact after the first vote. Keep the other assumptions fixed.

**Student question** [Ask who changed their choice and why.]

## Debrief

Trace the economic mechanism. Name an alternative explanation and the evidence that would distinguish it.

## Instructor note

Give a short timing plan, a likely disagreement and a follow-up for each side. Use the same opening, exhibit and prompts for presentation mode and the student handout. Hide the debrief until discussion.
