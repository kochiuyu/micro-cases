ACC-48 · Hong Kong · 15 minutes · Group audit · Aggregation risk · Revenue cutoff

The branches agree. Do the records?

The group auditor owns the conclusion

Hong Kong’s Accounting and Financial Reporting Council says that group auditors must direct, supervise and review component auditors’ work to support the group opinion. A fictional appliance repair group puts that principle to work when access to evidence changes at year end.

Your classroom decision

A Hong Kong group audits three depot components in Hong Kong and nearby cities. Their small fulfillment counters sell replacement kits; for this case revenue is earned when the customer physically receives the kit. All three counters use the same premature year-end posting rule. An internal workbook cannot establish the customer’s receipt date.

Overall group materiality is HK$600,000; group performance materiality is HK$400,000, used in planning to allow for combined undetected and uncorrected errors. The figures below are separate, same-direction possible overstatements for one population, not alternative estimates or a complete view of group risk.

Round 2 · More information

A component auditor retrieves dated customer acknowledgements for all South dispatches at issue. Subject to the group auditor reviewing the work, HK$160,000 was received after year end and HK$20,000 was received on time. The central audit team also finds HK$90,000 of inter-depot kit sales still in group revenue, a separate population with no overlap.

Your decision

How much identified overstatement affects consolidated revenue? Which corrections and follow-up work would you request before clearing the group audit?

Do not average away shared errors

The first scenario’s exposure is HK$270,000 known and up to HK$450,000 in total. The upper end exceeds planning performance materiality. Request direct receipt evidence or independently corroborated alternatives around year end at South. Review the scope, sample or complete population, dates, exceptions and conclusions of component work.

Test the central process as well

Trace local journals into the group ledger and test the booking rule and period-end consolidation. Look across all relevant components for the same risk, not just the one where documentation was harder to access. Where access remains limited after alternatives, escalate the evidence gap and assess audit reporting consequences; do not decide the opinion from the range alone.

Revised identified amounts

The cutoff error is 140 + 130 + 160 = HK$430,000. Add the separate HK$90,000 group revenue elimination, for HK$520,000 total consolidated revenue overstatement. Request corrections and verify they reach group reporting; separately assess related receivables, inventory, cost-of-sales and tax if relevant, rather than assuming a HK$520,000 profit effect.

A planning level does not decide the opinion

HK$520,000 is below overall materiality yet above performance materiality. Neither comparison is an automatic clearance rule. Review the remaining population and all other misstatements, evaluate qualitative significance, corroborate the adjustments and document whether group evidence is sufficient. Component performance materiality is a separate group-auditor determination when required, not a division of group materiality across the three depots.

Instructor note

Ask students to swap the order of the evidence: what would change if a clean component conclusion arrived first but the group auditor could not review the work? Separate identifying risk, obtaining appropriate evidence, and evaluating remaining misstatements.
