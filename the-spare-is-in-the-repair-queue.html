OPS-49 · Hong Kong · 15 minutes · repairable spares · replenishment · expected shortfall

The spare is in the repair queue

Inventory can come back from repair

HAECO has described an agreement combining component pooling and repair management. IATA industry guidance also describes arrangements in which removed parts are repaired and returned to a pool. Below, a fictional Hong Kong workshop makes only an inventory decision; it does not direct component work or flight decisions.

Your classroom decision

Jade Workshop holds two approved exchange units for a regional aviation customer. Both are issued against two confirmed requests in the first week. The two units surrendered in exchange go to the repair provider; only a unit certified and returned by the next weekly deadline can refill the ready stock.

Before week one, choose one option: base service, rent a third approved spare for HK$4,800, or buy priority repair for HK$2,900. The third spare sits ready and unused until week two. With priority service, the exhibit gives the total certified returns, not additional units on top of the base returns.

Week-two requests are known within each demand-and-return scenario in the exhibit. All unused ready units are interchangeable. Each unmet week-two request costs HK$9,000 in a fictional service credit. All other costs and outcomes are held equal.

Round 2 · More information

The provider revises its promise: in the congested scenario priority service would still return zero by the deadline. The other rows, base returns and fees are unchanged.

Your decision

Which option now has the lowest expected cost? What is the most you would pay to rent the third spare under this revised outlook?

First decision: treat the repair line as future stock

Base misses 0, 2 and 1 requests in order. The average is 0.30 × 2 + 0.20 × 1 = 0.8, which costs HK$7,200. The rented spare raises ready stock by one in every row, giving misses of 0, 1 and 0, or HK$2,700 in expected credits before the HK$4,800 fee.

Priority wins before the promise changes

Priority also gives misses of 0, 1 and 0. Its total is HK$2,700 + HK$2,900 = HK$5,600, versus HK$7,500 for the rented spare and HK$7,200 for base. An extra return in the ordinary row has no credit value: the only request is already covered.

Second decision: value the returns you can count on

Revised priority misses 0, 2 and 0. It costs 0.30 × 2 × HK$9,000 + HK$2,900 = HK$8,300. Base is now lowest. Rent avoids 0.5 expected unfilled requests relative to base: its fee can be at most 0.5 × HK$9,000 = HK$4,500.

What to take away

Late work-in-process cannot cover a current service request. Judge faster repair by what it returns in the rows where stock would otherwise be short.

Instructor note

Fictional inventory and service credits only. Ask why shortening average repair time can leave the worst shortages untouched. No physical repair, inspection or certification practice is taught.
