GVR-11 · Hong Kong · 10–15 minutes · Contract changes · Shared obligations · Change records

The meeting agreed. Which version applies?

In the news

In a written Legislative Council reply on 6 July 2016, Hong Kong’s Development Bureau described why public works designs sometimes need adjustment and how variation orders are monitored.

That historical public-works discussion is context, not a rule for every private contract. The supplier, agreement and change request below are invented.

Your classroom decision

Fictional classroom situation. Your Hong Kong exhibition supplier has an agreed HK$40,000 job for ten display panels, including a matte finish and installation. Production of the finish starts this afternoon; the frames can be made independently.

After a sample meeting, the customer’s design lead sends a genuine email: “We like the textured finish. Please use that version.” The designer can approve appearance, but the supplied project procedure reserves changes to price and installation arrangements for the customer’s project manager and your owner together. No revised package has been agreed.

Both parties have already allowed two routes: release the original matte finish today, or hold only the finish until tomorrow’s change discussion while the frames proceed. The hold uses a later production slot and leaves one rather than two days for the customer’s discretionary preview. Either route still meets the contracted installation deadline, with the same safety and quality checks. No extra hold charge applies.

The original specification remains the agreed fallback in this exercise. Today’s choice is how to manage the request and preserve the relationship, not whether to treat the designer’s preference as unlimited spending authority.

Your decision

Would you release the original finish or hold that portion for the change discussion? Explain the tradeoff and what you would tell the customer before production proceeds.

Round 2 · More information

For this second decision, assume the finish was held and the frames proceeded. If you chose A, consider this alternative branch: after matte production, a switch would require a separate rework proposal, not the price below.

The designer clarifies that the email approved appearance only. They assumed the texture had no effect on price or installation. The project manager has not approved either change and is available tomorrow morning.

Your production team now supplies a complete proposal: the textured version adds HK$4,000 to the total price and requires two extra installation hours. The venue can provide those hours in an available earlier access slot before the unchanged completion deadline. The customer must arrange a representative for that slot; availability is not yet confirmed. The price includes all additional supplier labour and materials, with no further quoted charge.

The customer likes the texture, but must decide whether it is worth the additional price, earlier attendance and shorter preview. The original finish remains acceptable under the agreed specification. Both authorised representatives can approve a complete change tomorrow, or confirm the original version. Nothing has yet been signed or charged for the proposed change.

Your decision

What exactly would you ask both authorised representatives to agree before releasing the textured finish? Recommend whether to pursue that change or retain the original, and state the fallback if agreement is not reached tomorrow.

Separate a preference from a complete package

The email resolves one question: which appearance the designer likes. It does not resolve the additional price or who will attend the earlier access slot. The missing agreement concerns connected obligations, not the authenticity of the sender.

The supplier should explain the consequences without blaming the designer. A genuine message can still leave important matters open.

Make the proposed change usable

Record the affected panels and finish sample/version; the proposed HK$44,000 total; the earlier access slot and responsible customer representative; the unchanged completion deadline; and how acceptance will be checked. Identify who confirms the package on each side and which original terms stay unchanged.

The extra HK$4,000 is a proposed price change, not a proven cost saving, profit or amount already owed. Forty thousand plus four thousand equals forty-four thousand; the two hours do not automatically extend the final deadline.

Choose a version, not an indefinite pause

The original remains defensible if the customer values preview time, cannot cover earlier access or declines the additional price. Pursuing texture is defensible if those consequences are acceptable and the complete package is agreed before release.

The one-day hold preserves a choice at a real scheduling cost. It is not permission to suspend the entire job indefinitely. If no change is agreed by tomorrow’s release point, use the already agreed original fallback and notify the team and customer.

Give everyone the same current instruction

Once a version is confirmed, link the change record to the original agreement and issue one current production and installation instruction. Retain the earlier messages as history rather than leaving competing versions in circulation.

This exercise supplies its own approval procedure. In a real disagreement, the actual contract, communications and applicable law need appropriate review; the case does not decide whether every informal message is legally binding.

Instructor note

Allow 2 minutes for the opening, 3 for the first vote, 2 for the reveal, 4 for a complete change record and revote, and 2 for synthesis. Compare releasing the agreed finish with holding it at the cost of a shorter preview.

Reset round two explicitly to the finish-held branch with frames proceeding, whatever students first chose. The HK$4,000 addition and two installation hours belong to that branch; do not price a switch after matte production without a separate rework proposal.

Ask students to record specification, proposed total price, access and attendance, preview timing, approval roles and the shared current instruction. A genuine design preference is not authority for every obligation. Do not turn the fictional procedure into a universal signature rule or legal conclusion.
