ENT-42 · Hong Kong · 15 minutes · Grant reimbursement · Cash runway · Supplier terms · Market testing

The grant pays later. Can the venture start now?

Approval and receipt are separate events

In written answers for the 2026–27 budget examination, Hong Kong’s Trade and Industry Department explained that eligible applicants in some BUD Fund categories may choose an initial payment after signing a funding agreement. The scheme’s guides also describe payments made after project reporting. This case uses a fictional grant with no initial payment and its own dates.

Your classroom decision

A Hong Kong reusable-packaging venture is choosing a two-market pilot supported by a fictional reimbursement grant or a smaller self-funded pilot in one market. Its founders have HK$110,000 in cash and require at least HK$35,000 left each week after all scheduled payments.

The grant pays HK$60,000 in week 7 after review under the initial forecast. The venture has no advance, loan or sales receipts in this nine-week window. It cannot run both pilots. Its rule is to test two markets if that is feasible without breaching the floor, and otherwise to run the smaller pilot.

Round 2 · More information

The main supplier now offers to move HK$30,000 of the week 3 bill to week 8 for a HK$4,000 surcharge paid with it. This does not change grant eligibility, the amount reimbursed or the work. The rest of the week 3 bill is still due then.

The revised plan must be tested with the same HK$60,000 arriving either as forecast in week 7, or two weeks late in week 9. The supplier’s new week 8 deadline does not move if the grant is delayed.

Your decision

Should the venture accept the supplier offer under its cash rule? Find the lowest cash balance in both grant scenarios and the extra committed cash needed to preserve the floor if the grant arrives late.

Debrief · Terminal cost does not tell you runway

Without new terms, the two-market pilot’s balances after the events are HK$74k, HK$22k, HK$10k and HK$70k. Its net cost of HK$40k is lower than the one-market pilot’s HK$60k, but cash falls HK$25k below the floor. The smaller pilot ends at HK$50k and never falls below it.

Debrief · Deferral buys time, not free money

With the supplier offer, balances in weeks 1, 3 and 5 are HK$74k, HK$52k and HK$40k. If the reimbursement arrives in week 7, cash rises to HK$100k and pays HK$34k in week 8, ending at HK$66k. The lowest balance is HK$40k: feasible.

Debrief · Test the date before committing

If reimbursement arrives in week 9 instead, the week 8 payment takes cash from HK$40k to HK$6k before it rises to HK$66k. The delayed case is short HK$29k relative to the floor. Identical ending cash conceals very different room to operate.

Debrief · Require a cash solution in advance

If the week 9 date remains possible and the floor is non-negotiable, choose the smaller pilot unless at least HK$29k of extra cash is committed and usable before the week 8 payment, ignoring its financing costs. A later negotiated supplier due date could also change the result, but it is not part of the offer.

Instructor note

Allow 15 minutes: 2 to read, 5 to build the first ledger, 4 to stress-test the revised terms, and 4 to discuss. Ask which piece of evidence on timing would justify revising the plan and what the founder might still owe even with a positive ending balance.

Sources and case assumptions · optional reference

All information needed for classroom decisions is provided above. Sources are optional reading for attribution and background.

All venture figures, project scopes, supplier terms and fictional grant rules are invented. The dated sources illustrate different award and disbursement structures only. They are not guidance on actual eligibility, deadlines or approval.

  1. Trade and Industry Department, replies for examination of the 2026–27 estimates. The official reply explains the separate funding agreement and initial payment stages for selected BUD categories.
  2. BUD E-commerce Easy Guide to Application, November 2025. Sections 5.1.2 to 5.1.4 distinguish optional initial disbursement from later reimbursement subject to reports.
  3. BUD Mainland and FTA programmes, sample final report, January 2026. The programme’s own sample shows the documentation and audited accounts associated with completion reporting. It is not the simulated grant contract.
  4. Trade and Industry Department, BUD Fund overview. Describes the real programme’s export-market development context and separate application routes.
  5. Innovation and Technology Fund, Enterprise Support Scheme FAQ. A distinct scheme illustrates matching contributions, staged payments and progress evidence. Its rules are not assigned to the simulated grant.
