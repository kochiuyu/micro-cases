ACC-10 · Hong Kong · 10–15 minutes · Accounting · Principal and agent · Gross and net revenue

Through our till. All our revenue?

In the news · Gross sales and commission income

Hong Kong Technology Venture’s 2025 annual report distinguishes gross sales as principal from net commission income for agency transactions. Its financial statements were approved on 30 March 2026.

This is an accounting example, not a claim of error or a change in policy. The print desk below is fictional and has its own supplied facts.

Your classroom decision

Fictional classroom situation. HarbourPrints, a Hong Kong art-print sales desk, has helped customers order 100 prints at HK$400 each from an independent maker. Every print has been delivered and accepted this month. The desk has received all HK$40,000 of customer payments.

A settlement schedule allocates HK$32,000 to the maker, still payable at month-end, and HK$8,000 to the desk. HarbourPrints separately incurred HK$3,000 of its own operating expenses for these transactions.

For this exercise, those are all its expenses and transactions: no tax, finance costs, refunds, unfinished services or opening balances complicate the comparison. Two draft reports await review of the agreements and actual fulfilment responsibilities. Nothing has been finalised or released.

Draft monthly reportA · Gross presentationB · Arranging-fee presentation
RevenueHK$40,000HK$8,000
Merchandise expenseHK$32,000None
Own operating expensesHK$3,000HK$3,000
Resulting case profitHK$5,000HK$5,000

Classroom principle: a business controlling a promised product before passing it to the customer reports the sale gross. If its promise is only to arrange another provider’s product, it reports its earned fee. Control concerns directing use and obtaining benefits, not simply collecting payment.

The two drafts are provisional analyses, not permitted alternatives from which to choose a flattering headline. Responsibility, inventory risk and pricing provide evidence; they are not a majority-vote checklist. Check the facts before approving either report.

Your decision

Which draft is your provisional starting point? What fact about the desk’s promise and control would change your analysis? Do not approve either for release without the facts.

Round 2 · More information

The accepted orders, disclosed desk terms and actual practice agree. Customers choose the maker’s specified prints. The maker accepts each order, sets the HK$400 price and is responsible for acceptable delivery and any required remedy.

The maker retains control until customer delivery. HarbourPrints cannot redirect or use the prints, replace the promised maker, or require the maker to fulfil on its own behalf. It never commits to purchase stock and bears no inventory risk before or after delivery.

The desk’s only promise is to arrange the orders and collect payment on the maker’s behalf for a fixed HK$80 fee per completed order. All 100 arrangement services are complete this month. No additional service or implied fulfilment promise changes that role.

These are checked fictional operating facts, not merely the label “agent”. The desk still has to perform its arranging obligation responsibly.

Your decision

Which presentation should now be finalised? Reconcile the HK$40,000 collected to the desk’s revenue and amount owed, and explain why its profit did not drop by HK$32,000.

The supplied facts support fee revenue

B is the supported presentation. The desk arranges the maker’s product rather than controlling that product before delivery. Its completed arranging fees are 100 × HK$80 = HK$8,000 revenue.

The remaining 100 × HK$320 = HK$32,000 is payable to the maker. Together, HK$8,000 plus HK$32,000 reconcile to the HK$40,000 customer receipts. Under this agency arrangement, the maker’s amount is not the desk’s merchandise expense.

A smaller revenue line does not remove profit twice

The provisional gross draft showed HK$40,000 − HK$32,000 − HK$3,000 = HK$5,000. The supported fee presentation shows HK$8,000 − HK$3,000 = HK$5,000.

Correcting that draft removes HK$32,000 from both revenue and merchandise expense. It does not deduct HK$32,000 again from profit. Equal profit does not make the two revenue presentations equally correct.

These are the exercise’s complete-period figures, not closing bank balances. We know receipts and the settlement payable, but have not been told whether the desk’s own expenses have been paid.

Follow the promise, not the payment route

Collecting the customer’s money does not establish control over the print. Here the maker’s fulfilment responsibility and retained control, together with the desk’s actual arranging-only role, support the result.

This does not make every intermediary an agent. A business can control a product while using another supplier to fulfil it. Real arrangements need examination of all relevant promises and practices, not a count of indicators or the word “commission”.

Fee accounting is not permission to ignore customer problems or a conclusion that legal duties disappear.

A genuine choice remains in the internal dashboard

After recording revenue correctly, the owner can lead the internal dashboard with HK$8,000 earned fee revenue to discuss own income, showing HK$40,000 completed customer transaction value beside it.

Alternatively, lead with HK$40,000 completed customer transaction value handled to discuss activity, immediately showing the HK$8,000 fee and HK$32,000 settlement reconciliation. The HK$40,000 must not be labelled revenue or retained cash.

Both presentations show HK$3,000 own expenses and HK$5,000 case profit when discussing profit. The emphasis depends on the management question; it does not change the accounting or establish which headline an external audience prefers.

Instructor note

Round one asks for a provisional analysis and missing evidence, not a discretionary accounting-policy election. After the reveal, require B with the receipt-to-fee-and-payable reconciliation. Do not manufacture a second valid classification from the same supplied facts.

Ask why equal bottom lines can hide a material reporting difference. Keep the stated gross draft in view: it already included the HK$32,000 expense, so removing both lines leaves profit unchanged. Do not import a correction for a different original entry.

The short dashboard discussion is a real managerial choice, constrained by accurate labels and immediate reconciliation. This is an original bounded accounting exercise, not professional advice, an investment claim or a claim about the local report’s correctness.
