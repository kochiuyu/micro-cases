ACC-01 · Hong Kong · 10–15 minutes · Sunk cost · Opportunity cost

The last tray of egg tarts

Hong Kong in the news · June 2024

Wan Chai volunteers collected unsold bread from bakeries at closing time for redistribution. For a baker facing an offer at the door, what other use does the last tray have tonight?

Your classroom decision

You run a Hong Kong bakery. Twelve egg tarts sit on the counter as you turn the sign to Closed. The café next door offers to take the whole tray. You have no other buyer or use for it tonight.

Closing ledger, HK$ per tart · classroom assumptions
Already spent on baking8
Café offer6
Extra cost of selling0
Your decision

Would you sell all twelve? Show the difference tonight’s choice makes to the till.

Round 2 · One fact changes

Your pastry chef checks tomorrow’s orders. Keeping the tray for bread pudding would save HK$7 per tart in other ingredients, after extra preparation costs. Demand for pudding is already confirmed.

Your decision

Does the café get the tray now? What offer would make you indifferent?

Debrief · Compare the choices

Sell in round one. Both choices leave the HK$96 baking bill paid. Selling brings in HK$72 more than discarding. In round two, keeping the tray saves HK$84. Selling for HK$72 would give up HK$12 of value.

Instructor note

Minutes 0–3: vote and compare reasons in pairs. Minutes 3–7: reveal the changed fact and vote again. Minutes 7–12: reconcile the answers. The break-even offer is HK$7 per tart. Ask students who chose HK$8 what changes in the till when yesterday’s bill is entered on both sides. Ask those who chose HK$7 how an uncertain pudding order would change the saving.
