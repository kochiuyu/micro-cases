ENT-48 · Hong Kong · 15 minutes · Software pricing · Customer cohorts · Contribution · Support commitments

One payment. How much support follows?

Software pricing and service entitlements can differ

Microsoft’s 2024 Office licensing material distinguishes a one-time purchase from an ongoing subscription that includes additional services. Other providers set their own use and support terms. The fictional Hong Kong software business below prices a different service; it does not copy any provider’s plan.

Your classroom decision

Ming’s Hong Kong team is launching a desktop inventory notebook. It can offer a single purchase for HK$300 with 36 months of opt-in help, or a cancellable monthly plan for HK$18 with help only while paid. A purchase includes continued use of that version, without the staffed help after month 36. Both offers have the same functions during the measurement period; choose one for new customers.

The forecasts assume 100 customers begin under either offer and measure the first 36 months only. A support account-month means one account uses staffed help during one month; count it once regardless of contacts. At the stated monthly offer, the team expects every paid account-month to use support once or more. All other costs and receipts are identical across plans, and no new buyers beyond this cohort are counted.

Round 2 · More information

A longer replay of comparable usage suggests the one-time offer will create 2,500 support account-months, not 1,600. These are the only revised inputs. No offer has been made, and the team would honour the support term under either plan.

Your decision

Which plan now gives higher contribution? At what number of one-time support account-months do the two offers tie, holding the other forecasts fixed?

Debrief · One fee pays for many months

The one-time plan collects 100 × HK$300 = HK$30,000 in purchase receipts. Removing 1,600 × HK$5 leaves HK$22,000 contribution. Monthly receipts are 1,400 × HK$18 = HK$25,200; less HK$7,000 for support gives HK$18,200. Choose one-time under the initial forecast.

Debrief · Contribution is not cash on a date

The one-time buyers pay at purchase, while monthly collections depend on paid months. The period totals do not provide dated support spending or another month’s cash balance; build a separate cash schedule if timing matters.

Debrief · Identify the service exposure

At 2,500 support account-months, one-time contribution falls to HK$30,000 − HK$12,500 = HK$17,500. Monthly remains HK$18,200 and leads by HK$700. One-time ties at (HK$30,000 − HK$18,200) ÷ HK$5 = 2,360 support account-months.

Debrief · Clarify the promise before selling

State separately how long the software can be used, how long help is included, and what happens on cancellation. Check whether buyer counts, use, churn, refunds or wages would differ in practice. Do not change an existing support promise to improve this comparison.

Instructor note

Allow 15 minutes: 2 to read, 4 to compute, 4 to test the changed forecast, and 5 to discuss what would make the cohort comparable. The exercise describes operating receipts less support costs, not accounting revenue recognition.
