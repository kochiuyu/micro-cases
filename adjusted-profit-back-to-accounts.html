ACC-41 · Hong Kong · 15 minutes · HKFRS 18 · Management-defined performance measures · Financial reporting · Income tax and non-controlling interests

Adjusted profit. Can readers get back to the accounts?

Hong Kong changes how companies explain adjusted profit

The Hong Kong Institute of Certified Public Accountants issued HKFRS 18 in July 2024. It applies to annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027; early application is permitted. Qualifying management-defined profit measures used in public communications now have to be explained and reconciled in a note when the company applies the standard.

Your classroom decision

Ivy prepares the annual investor slides for a fictional Hong Kong-listed parcel group. It is applying HKFRS 18 for its first full year, 2027. The financial statements show HK$30m in group operating profit. The draft investor deck calls HK$38m “underlying operating profit” after adding back the cost of reorganising depots.

Management uses the adjusted subtotal to track how depot operations perform before a defined group of restructuring costs. The subtotal covers the group as a whole and is not a specified HKFRS subtotal. The slides will be posted publicly for investors.

The parent’s restructuring expense generates no income tax benefit. The subsidiary’s expense is deductible at a hypothetical 20% statutory tax rate. Assume no deferred tax effects. Outside shareholders own 20% of the subsidiary and share proportionately in its after-tax result. All figures and tax assumptions are fictional.

Current year, HK$mAmount
HKFRS operating profit30
Parent’s depot restructuring expense2
Subsidiary’s depot restructuring expense6
Your decision

Would you clear the HK$38m for the public slides as drafted? If the group keeps the measure, what reconciliation and separate tax and outside-shareholder effects would you put in the financial statement note?

Round 2 · More information

Ivy finds the 2026 records. Using the same HKFRS 18 presentation basis, group operating profit was HK$34m. It included HK$0.5m of comparable depot restructuring costs at the parent and HK$1.5m at the same subsidiary. The tax treatment and ownership shares were identical.

The communications lead proposes calling the exclusions “one-off costs” and publishing just the percentage increase in adjusted profit. Assume the comparative data are complete and can be checked.

Your decision

Using the same calculation for both years, how did reported and adjusted operating profit move? What would you tell the communications lead to publish, rename or drop?

Debrief · Identify the measure

Do not clear the draft as written. The public slides use a non-specified subtotal of income and expenses to show management’s view of group performance. It qualifies as a management-defined performance measure (MPM). Keep HK$30m as HKFRS operating profit; the separate bridge is HK$30m + HK$2m + HK$6m = HK$38m, before tax.

Debrief · Show effects by item

For the HK$2m parent add-back, both effects are zero: the cost is non-deductible and did not affect outside shareholders. For the HK$6m subsidiary add-back, the hypothetical tax expense rises by HK$1.20m (20% × HK$6m) because the adjustment removes a deductible expense.

The subsidiary add-back therefore raises its hypothetical after-tax result by HK$4.80m. Non-controlling interests’ share rises by HK$0.96m (20% × HK$4.80m). Show these effects alongside the adjustment, not as deductions from the HK$38m pre-tax subtotal. No actual tax liability or profit attribution changes.

Debrief · Compare on the same basis

In 2026 the bridge is HK$34m + HK$0.5m + HK$1.5m = HK$36m; show zero tax and outside-shareholder effects for the parent item and a HK$0.30m tax expense increase and HK$0.24m outside-shareholder increase for the subsidiary item.

Reported operating profit fell from HK$34m to HK$30m, about 11.8%. Adjusted operating profit rose from HK$36m to HK$38m, about 5.6%. Both statements can be true because the expenses excluded in the adjusted measure rose from HK$2m to HK$8m.

Debrief · Name what you excluded

Ivy should reject “one-off”: these costs occurred in both years. If the subtotal helps readers track depot activity before these costs, label it “operating profit before depot restructuring costs”, explain what qualifies as a restructuring cost and why the measure is useful, and show both trends. An add-back does not remove an expense from the financial statements.

Put the measure in the single MPM note with its calculation, the reconciliation to operating profit, the tax method and per-item tax and non-controlling interest effects, and comparable prior-year information. Say it reflects management’s view and similarly named measures at other companies may not be comparable.

Instructor note

Allow 15 minutes: 3 to locate the two profit measures, 4 to do the tax and ownership calculations, 4 to compare trends, and 4 to draft a two-sentence investor message.

Recurring costs are not automatically barred from an adjustment. Ask whether the label and reason imply that costs will never recur, and what readers would miss if only the adjusted trend were shown. The case assumes a qualifying public written deck and a full comparative period; it is not reporting or tax advice.
